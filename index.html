Teaching technology, one learner at a time

I am an EdTech Tutor i teach basic tech skills to both children and adults. I believe digital skills are not just for a select few — they are tools anyone can pick up and use to solve real problems.

Alongside tutoring, I design and build coding, robotics and AI curriculum for primary and secondary school students, and I develop modern, responsive websites for individuals and small businesses. Whatever the project, my goal is the same: help people use technology to create, solve problems and grow.

  • Certified EdTech tutor with a focus on coding and robotics
  • Frontend developer building fast, responsive websites
  • Curriculum designer for Scratch, robotics and AI programs
  • Mentor supporting learners from first line of code to first job
